Why this fundraiser

My name is Federico Solinas and I am officially recognised as 80% disabled.

I gave what I had to the fundraiser for my friend Francesco, 19, who has chondrosarcoma, for his trip to Florence for treatment. I did it together with some associations, putting Francesco's health before my own needs.

Now I need 1500 € for food and to take care of my animals.

The 1500 € will cover food, the upkeep of the animals (budgies, aquariums, rabbits), rent and medication for pain therapy.

I kept working in a kitchen against medical advice: this led to a hamstring tendon injury and, because I did not stop, a partial tendon detachment. In the end my contract was not renewed. I have an exemption, but I still pay prescription charges on my medication.

I sent three certified emails (PEC) to the social services of my town. The only reply was: “It does you credit that you want to work.” That was the help I received.

At home with me there are two aquariums, two budgies and two rabbits. My ex-partner left them with me when she said she had to renovate her house, and she never came back. The animals are doing really well: I have always put them first, even when I went without eating.

I don’t like asking for help. When I go back to work, I will pay everything back.

Thank you from the bottom of my heart.

— Federico

My story

In 2012, in Florence, I had surgery for a rare malignant tumour, clear cell chondrosarcoma: it was the first case in my region. They removed the head and neck of my femur and implanted a megaprosthesis in my left hip.

In July 2021 I had surgery on my right hip at the Rizzoli in Bologna, for hip osteoarthritis and CAM-type femoroacetabular impingement. It was the middle of the pandemic and I paid out of my own pocket, because if I had waited I would have risked needing a prosthesis on the right side too. The operation was a success and the osteoarthritis stopped progressing.

In December 2023 I had another operation on my left hip for metallosis and a pseudotumour: the titanium stem stayed, and the metal head was replaced with a ceramic one. The prognosis was six months; after one month I was already walking, because my only thought was getting back to work in the kitchen and not living off anyone.

I was renting with my ex-partner, Maria, and we split the rent, the groceries and the costs of the animals she had wanted. At the first small financial difficulty of my life I took out a loan, so she wouldn't have to pay her half of the rent. We had decided to tighten our belts: I stopped buying anything for myself and thought only about the home and the animals. Then I found out she had bought herself the latest high-end appliances. Shortly afterwards she said she had to go and renovate a house she owned, and she never came back.

I found myself alone with the rent, the household bills, the animals and the loan repayments. I used my savings, then my credit card, and my account went into the red. I had to choose between myself and the animals, and my standing with the banks: I chose the lives of the animals, who are not to blame for anything.

In June 2026 I went back to work in a restaurant against medical advice. After a month the tendon gave way and became inflamed. I was advised to stop for a month, but I refused on my own responsibility and carried on through excruciating pain and heavy treatments. The workload increased, and I, who have always been fast as a cook, sped up. After almost another month the tendon stopped working: the MRI showed a partial detachment and an extensive tear. I was forced to stop, and my contract was not renewed because I am still on sick leave.

Everything I have, I earned by working, without ever asking anyone for help. Two weeks ago a 19-year-old boy, Francesco, received the same diagnosis as me: it was the second case. Even before opening the fundraiser for him I had already given him everything I could; then I opened the fundraiser, which reached its goal. I don't want to be helped: I help others when I can, and I am paying off my debts with my own money.

This is Federico: a human being, not an algorithm.

My account today

This is my current account today. Since my ex-partner left, I have had to go into debt on my credit card to pay the rent, the house and the animals. I have mobility problems and I live in the countryside: I haven't left the house for a month, because I don't have a single euro.

I have always helped everyone, even when I had nothing. I know some people may not believe me, because I put my face out there to help Francesco, but I have every document that proves the truth.

The person who told me «I love you for better and for worse» disappeared precisely when things got worse.

I also have a daughter, Alice, from a previous relationship: her mother is not my ex-partner. I met Alice when she was already four years old and I have seen her only twice; then her mother found another partner and disappeared. To spare the little girl from going through the courts, two years ago I started sending her €600 every month by instant bank transfer, more than half of my salary. Until one day the transfer came back: her mother had changed city, account and phone number.

I no longer know if helping is the right thing. I believed love showed itself in hard times. I don't ask for anything: I'm glad you helped Francesco.
